<Steps>
  <Step title="Officer presence" icon="user-shield">
    - The presence of a correctional officer works as a deterrent, and can diffuse a situation.
    - Officers should keep professional and a nonthreatening demeanor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verbal Commands" icon="comment">
    - Issuing calm, nonthreatening commands.
    - Officers may increase their volume and shorten commands in an attempt to gain compliance.
    - Short commands might include "Stop," or "Don't move."
    - If nonthreatening commands don't work a warning can follow. For example: "Don't move or you'll be tased".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Empty Hand Control" icon="hand-fist">
    - Soft techniques include grabs, holds and joint locks (including tackling) to restrain an individual.
    - Hard techniques include punches and kicks for defence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Less-Lethal Methods" icon="bolt">
    - Blunt impact, such as nightsticks
    - Taser Deployment
    - Beanbag Deployment
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lethal Force" icon="gun">
    Officers can use deadly weapons such as firearms to stop an individual's actions only under certain conditions:

    - Lethal force may only be used when the individual is presenting an immediate threat to life, whether that be your own, another officer's, or an innocent third party.
    - A warning shot should be issued where possible.
    - Any use of lethal force must follow with an incident report being written.
  </Step>
</Steps>
