Inmates held until trial (HUT) are those awaiting a court hearing before their sentence is finalised. They must be handled with additional care and coordination with the Department of Justice and FIB.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm HUT status" icon="clipboard-check">
    On intake, verify with PD/FIB that the inmate is marked as HUT. Log this in the appropriate channel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign secure cell" icon="lock">
    HUT inmates are typically placed in Medium or High Security depending on FIB/DOJ classification.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Restrict visitation" icon="user-slash">
    All visitations for HUT inmates must be approved by FIB/DOJ in advance. Do not allow unapproved visitors.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Remove communication devices" icon="phone">
    Phones, radios, and any communication devices must be confiscated and logged, unless FIB/DOJ specifies otherwise.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Adjust jail time as needed" icon="clock">
    HUT inmates may have their time adjusted via `/jail hut` when running low. Coordinate with a Sergeant+ before making changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>
