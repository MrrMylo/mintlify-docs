Procedures for handling inmates sentenced to Maximum Security at the San Andreas Department of Corrections.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Food and water only">
    If a person is sentenced to Maximum Security they are only to be given food and water.
  </Step>

  <Step title="No normal visitation or phone privileges">
    They will NOT receive normal visitation or phone privileges!

    Unless it is a Lawyer or Psychiatrist who has scheduled an appointment with a Judge, DOC Command, and the Client.

    - If an appointment has been scheduled, a Judge will release the Inmate temporarily and the inmate will be escorted to visitation with an armed escort.
    - During the interview an ARMED guard is to be stationed nearby.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Corrections Officer must be present">
    No person(s) should enter Maximum Security without a Corrections Officer present.
  </Step>
</Steps>
