Procedures for conduct at Police Stations and City Hall while representing the San Andreas Department of Corrections.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Be professional at all times">
    Be professional at all times.

    - Do not tase each other or other officers.
    - While out in public, all weapons should remain holstered and your specialist weapons (SMG/Rifle) should remain in your vehicle.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Police Stations">
    Only be there for on-duty reasons. Transports would be the main reason, but some exceptions can be made as long as it pertains to the job.

    - Try not to loiter, it is a privilege to be allowed there, it can be removed at any time.
    - Do not disrupt the work of the officers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="City Hall">
    Keep peace, remove people from the property if asked too, call police if the situation escalates.

    - You should only be here with an official purpose and permission of a state representative (includes DOJ).
    - Never pull a firearm, unless you are defending yourself.
    - Listen to the state employees.
  </Step>
</Steps>
