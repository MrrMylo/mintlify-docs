While following the use of force procedures, when giving orders to inmates you must also utilise the 'Ask, Tell, Make' model of escalating force. It follows a simple basis to allow the inmate to comply before force is used. First you ASK the individual to follow your order, then you TELL the individual to follow your order, and then you MAKE the individual to follow your order.

## Example

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask" icon="circle-question">
    CO: “Can you turn around”

    Inmate: “No”
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tell" icon="circle-exclamation">
    CO: “I’m ordering you to turn around”

    Inmate: “No”
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make" icon="hand-fist">
    CO: “if you don’t turn around you’ll be tackled and cuffed”
  </Step>
</Steps>

Following this dialogue, you may follow the escalation of force to 'make' the inmate follow your order by using a reasonable level of force.
