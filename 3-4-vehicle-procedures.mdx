<Warning>
  You are not a police officer. Your lights and sirens should NEVER be used to rush somewhere. The ONLY exception for the use of your lights and sirens is for a prison transport. Any traffic violations reported to Command by a police officer will result in a punishment, up to termination depending on the severity.
</Warning>

## Vehicle Customisation

When purchasing any DOC vehicle, you must go to a Customs Shop & make the following changes:

- Add the DOC livery.
- Change the body color to Steel Grey or Black.
- Ensure all extras with internal lights are added.
- Ram bar is up to officers discretion.

A light bar may be added, provided that the vehicle will ONLY be used for transporting inmates to the prison. Other trips must be made in a vehicle that does not have a light bar installed.

<Note>
  You do not need to purchase any performance upgrades for the vehicle; they are installed, even if they don't show.
</Note>

## Permitted Use

You may not patrol around the city or county in your vehicle. These vehicles may only be used for essential trips to the tech store (smartwatch), grocery store (food) etc, or primarily transports to the prison. When exiting your vehicle, ensure your SMG/Rifle/Shotgun is in the glovebox and is not visible to the public unless conducting a transport.
