Visitation is allowed for regular inmates. For HUT inmates, these visitations must be approved by FIB/DOJ first.

<Note>
  All visitors must have a valid (not expired) state issued identification in order to enter the prison. Visitors are not allowed to have masks inside the prison.
</Note>

## Searching Visitors

All visitors need to be searched before entering the prison area. Bring the visitor into the airlock hallway and perform the search. If they don't approve of the search, they will not be allowed into the prison.

<Warning>
  No weapons, alcohol, drugs (including weed), or any form of contraband are allowed to be brought into the prison. If any illegal items are found on the visitor, turn the visitor away from property and notify PD immediately. They will be considered trespassing if they refuse to leave.
</Warning>

Keep in mind state workers may have registered firearms that you are not able to legally purchase. You can confiscate these weapons, but return them when they leave. EMS, DOJ, and State workers on official visitations are allowed to keep their less-lethal weapons for self defence.

## Logging Confiscated Items

All visitor items confiscated will be logged and log how much money they started with in the `#visitation-log`. The items will be placed into the evidence locker and will be retrieved from the evidence locker and returned to the visitor when they are leaving. Evidence is logged.

<Warning>
  DO NOT take anything out of the evidence locker unless it is returnable to the visitor.
</Warning>

## Searching Inmates

Inmates are to be given a full search before being allowed to enter the visitation center. Take any potential contraband if they still have any on them (phones, radios, drugs, weapons, etc.)

## Escorting Visitors

After the visitors are finished with being searched, a Correctional Officer will escort the visitors to the visitation area. When visitors are leaving, give them another search and log the amount of cash they are leaving with. You can give confiscated items back.
