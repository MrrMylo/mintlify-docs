<Info>
  **Uniform Guidelines**

  - This applies to all members of the Emergency Response Team.
  - You may only wear the DOC T-Shirt while on ERT Patrol, or while participating in training exercises.
  - All ERT Uniforms (except patrol) must contain helmet and mask.
  - Do not call out an officer by name if they have full tactical gear with a mask in order to prevent retaliation.
  - If you are told to change something about your uniform by a Supervisor, you must do so, or face warnings/strikes.
</Info>
