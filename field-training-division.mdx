<Info>
  **Uniform Guidelines**

  These uniforms have been created to ensure DOC Recruits can distinguish training officers whilst on duty. These also allow you to stand out as someone who can assist other officers if Supervisors are not available.

  In addition to the set out guidelines, please keep this in mind:

  - You may wear accessories with your uniform, however, please ensure they are kept to a minimum and professional.
  - You must wear your DOC badge around your neck or DOC Vest at all times.
  - Whilst wearing your FTP uniform, you are representing the division. Ensure you remain respectful and professional at all times.
  - You may wear any authorized hat as long as it looks professional. Glasses are permitted, but goggles (Items 37 and 52) are not.
</Info>

<Note>
  Rank: Field Training Division - Recruit+
</Note>
