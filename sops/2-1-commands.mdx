Here is a list of all the /commands you can use as a Department of Corrections Officer as well as a simple description of the function of the command. Anyone found abusing commands will be subject to discipline.

<Accordion title="/Impound">
  You make the nearest vehicle disappear for the cost of \$200.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Dispatch">
  Used to send Messages over dispatch that PD and DOC can both see. Always send a dispatch when DOC is on duty or when you are 10-7, 10-8 or when off duty to keep them informed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Search">
  Lets you perform a Search on the nearest person.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Softcuff & /sc">
  Allows you to place the inmate in soft cuffs. They are still able to walk and run, however not throw punches, etc.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Hardcuff & /hc">
  Allows you to place the inmate in hard cuffs. This means that the inmate can not do anything.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Uncuff & /uc">
  You remove the cuffs from a cuffed individual.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Escort">
  Lets you escort a hard-cuffed inmate.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Suspect [in,out] [Seat ID] & /Sus [in,out] [Seat ID]">
  Lets you move a hard-cuffed inmate in or out of a vehicle to specific seats.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Jail [server id] [amount]">
  Used to sentence someone to jail for the amount of months (months=minutes) that you listed. This would be regarding what they were charged with and what PD said their time would be after processing their charges. This should ONLY be used after you've completed a transport to the prison and you're booking them into jail.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Check_jail [server id]">
  Allows you to check the remaining time an inmate has left on their sentencing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Modify_jail [server id] [add/sub] [time]">
  Allows you to modify the remaining time an inmate has left of their sentence. This is limited to +/-5 months.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Fine [server id] [amount]">
  You should NOT be fining people typically. Let PD handle the fines and make sure they are fined before you transport the inmate. The ONLY time you will be allowed to fine someone is if a judge permits you to fine and process someone after a bench trial/court case.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Repair">
  Lets you repair your vehicle if you are at a government property. Our repair spot is where you pull out the bus/van.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Unit_ID">
  Change the callsign attached to your tracker. Make sure to do so every time your callsign changes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Carry">
  Lets you carry a person around.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Tracker">
  Turns your tracker on or off (your tracker should never be off). Add 'yes' to send an alert to DOC/PD when your tracker is broken. Only use this when necessary.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Training">
  Toggles your training status on your tracker.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Freq [1-10]">
  Changes your radio to the frequency that you input.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Radio_Volume">
  Adjusts your radio volume.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="/Drive_Mode [N/S/T]">
  Changes the way the vehicles handle, only applies to certain cars.
</Accordion>
