Below is a list of equipment and vehicles that you can use. Be advised that you can only select from the list below unless otherwise specified.

## Equipment

Below is a list of the standardized equipment that you must carry during an active shift:

<Info>
  - Phone
  - Radio
  - Smart Watch
  - Coil Stun Gun (Taser)
  - VF Combat Pistol
  - Nightstick/Flashlight
  - DOC Badge
  - Government Issued ID
  - Pistol Ammo
  - First Aid Kits/IFAKs
  - Body Armour
  - Photo Camera
</Info>

Below is a list of recommended, but not required, additions to the standardized equipment:

<Info>
  - Flight Radar
  - Empty Evidence Bags
  - Utility Belt
</Info>

## Vehicles

Depending on what rank you hold or division you are in, you will get access to different vehicles. You are not allowed to change the cosmetic appearance of the vehicles with the exception of the liveries. All of the custom vehicles are fitted with extras that can be added and removed. You should have all inside lights fitted, and no rooftop extras added.

Below is a list of the vehicles that are authorized to be used:

<Accordion title="Corrections Cadet+" icon="user-graduate">
  - Prison Bus
  - Eagle Pathmaker (Ford Explorer)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Sr. Corrections Officer+ and/or ERT Personnel" icon="user-shield">
  - Chevrox Taiga (Tahoe)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Sergeant+ and ERT Supervisor+ (must hold both positions)" icon="user-tie">
  - 2014 Thunder striker ('14 Charger)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Lieutenant+" icon="shield-halved">
  - MH6 Little Bird under conditions:
    - Civilian helicopter licence
    - An LEO helicopter pilot not available
    - There is an emergency situation demanding a helicopter (you should not leave the prison airspace)
  - Slam 35HD (Dodge Ram)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Captain+ and/or ERT Command" icon="star">
  - 2018 Thunder striker ('18 Charger)
</Accordion>
