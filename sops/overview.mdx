This is the official handbook of the San Andreas Department of Corrections. This document is written with the intent to improve and balance the current state of the San Andreas Department of Corrections. Everything in this document is subject to change over time and all officers are thereby responsible to be knowledgeable of Standard Operating Procedures at all times. Any changes to the SOPs will be noted in this document as well as announced through the proper channels.
